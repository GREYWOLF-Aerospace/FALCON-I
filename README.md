## Technical Specifications

The **FALCON-I** is a high-speed, scratch-built experimental scale RC aircraft. It is engineered around an advanced integrated propulsion duct system and a multi-axis high-lift control surface layout to study level transonic airflow behaviors, intake fluid dynamics.

* **Total Length of Aircraft:** 1.25 meters
* **Total Height of Aircraft (no landing Gear):** 0.3m (~30cm)
* **Fuselage Hull Height:** 0.1m (~11cm) *(Optimized to wrap tightly around the internal engine bay)*
* **Wingspan:** >~1.0 meter
* **Wing Area:** ~0.17 m²
* **Total Control Channels:** 4 Channels (2x Elevons, 2x Rudders)
* **Performance Profile:** Estimated empty airframe weight ranges from 2.9 to 3.1 kg. Powered by a high-output 6s electric ducted fan producing between 3.8 - 4 kgs of static thrust, yielding a >1:1 thrust-to-weight ratio for a flight envelope targeting 100-140 kph.

## Airframe Materials List

The structural framework utilizes a lightweight, high-strength composite prototyping approach to handle structural stress thresholds while maximizing the power-to-weight ratio:

1. **Simple PLA:** Used for the aerodynamic outer skin contours, wing surfacing, and lightweight internal fuselage formers.
2. **Carbon Fiber  Rods 3mm|5mm| :** Integrated directly into high-stress structural joints, serving as the main wing load spars, tail pivot shafts, and longitudinal fuselage stiffeners.

## Success Criteria & Flight Goals

To rigorously evaluate the airframe engineering, the testing program focuses on three definitive goals:
1. **Aerodynamic Validation:** Confirm stable, level cruise tracking at a 23 m/s airspeed vector to ground-truth our baseline steady-state CFD dataset.
2. **High speed:** Verify the behavior at high speeds of 100 - 140 kph.
